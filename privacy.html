Privacy Policy (English)

Effective: 1 August 2026

Sanmaru ("the Service") is a free, ad-free guide to Korea's famous mountains. This policy covers both the website and the Android app.

1. What we collect and why

  • Email address and nickname — for your account, to show you on the leaderboard and next to your posts.
  • Location (latitude, longitude, accuracy) — captured only at the moment you tap "verify climb". We never track your location in the background. The coordinate is stored whether verification succeeds or fails, so that we can detect and correct wrong summit coordinates; only the operator can read these records.
  • Climb records (mountain, verification method, timestamp) — for your log, points, badges and ranking.
  • Photos you upload — shown publicly on the mountain page.
  • Reviews, ratings, companion posts and suggestions, including any contact details you choose to type into a companion post.
  • Push notification token — only if you allow notifications on the website.
  • Website analytics (pages viewed, browser/device type, approximate region) via Google Analytics — on the website only. The Android app contains no analytics SDK.

We do not collect government IDs or payment information, and we never sell your data or use it for advertising.

2. Retention

We keep your account data until you delete your account. Content you delete is removed immediately.

3. Processors

Google LLC (Firebase — authentication, database, photo storage, notifications; data is stored outside Korea), Google Analytics (website statistics only), Netlify (web hosting), Open-Meteo (weather — we send only the mountain's coordinates, never yours), OpenStreetMap (map tiles).

4. Your rights

You may access, correct or delete your data at any time. You can edit your nickname and delete your own photos, reviews and posts in the app. For full account deletion, email us and we will process it.

5. Children

The Service is not directed at children under 14 and we do not knowingly collect their data.
