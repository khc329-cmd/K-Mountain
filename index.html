Frequently Asked Questions

Got a question? We've got answers.

How do I track my climbs?

Find a mountain you've climbed, tap "Mark as Climbed", and choose GPS verification or self-report. Your climb is logged instantly.

Is my data saved if I clear my browser?

Yes! Your climbs are saved in your browser's local storage and persist unless you manually clear site data.

Do I need to download an app?

No! Sanmaru is a PWA that works in your browser. You can add it to your home screen for instant access.

Can I use it offline?

Yes! Once you load the app, it works offline. You can browse mountains and mark climbs without internet.

How are points calculated?

Easy = 10 pts, Moderate = 25 pts, Hard = 50 pts. Bonus points for reviews, photos, and ratings.
